About Acreo Partners

Acreo Partners is an independent advisory firm supporting companies, investors, sponsors and family offices across strategic transactions, investment structuring, technology and corporate development.

We combine international transaction experience, investment insight and specialist technology expertise to help clients evaluate opportunities, navigate complexity and execute their strategic objectives.

Our work ranges from M&A and investment advisory to technology due diligence, value-creation planning and the coordination of Luxembourg investment and corporate structures.

Acreo Partners operates through a collaborative model, bringing together the appropriate legal, tax, regulatory, financial and industry specialists for each engagement. This enables our clients to access coordinated expertise while maintaining a single strategic point of contact.

We work selectively and confidentially, focusing on mandates where our experience, international network and hands-on involvement can create meaningful value.

Independent thinking. Coordinated execution. Lasting value.

Luxembourg Fund & Corporate Structuring Support

From investment concept to operational structure

Discuss your project See how it works

Acreo Partners supports international investors, sponsors, entrepreneurs and family offices in assessing, structuring and establishing Luxembourg investment and corporate vehicles.

We act as a central strategic coordinator throughout the process, helping clients translate their commercial and investment objectives into a clear implementation roadmap.

Working alongside selected Luxembourg legal advisers, tax specialists, AIFMs, central administrators, depositaries, banks, auditors and corporate service providers, we coordinate the different workstreams required to establish a professionally structured and operational vehicle.

Who we support

International investors and sponsors
Family offices and private investment groups
Entrepreneurs establishing a Luxembourg holding or investment platform
Companies creating cross-border ownership or acquisition structures
Promoters considering a Luxembourg investment fund
Existing structures requiring governance or operational support

Our services

Initial assessment

Understanding the proposed strategy, asset classes, investor profile, target jurisdictions, governance requirements and long-term objectives.

Structuring roadmap

Coordinating a preliminary assessment of potential vehicles, governance arrangements, service-provider requirements, implementation stages, indicative timing and costs.

Service-provider selection

Supporting the identification and coordination of appropriate legal, tax, regulatory, banking, administration, audit, domiciliation and governance providers.

Establishment and onboarding

Coordinating incorporation or establishment workstreams, documentation, KYC and AML requirements, banking arrangements and operational readiness.

Governance support

Assisting with governance design, board composition, reporting arrangements, conflicts frameworks and coordination between stakeholders and service providers.

Ongoing strategic coordination

Supporting the structure following launch, including strategic initiatives, transactions, service-provider coordination and governance-related projects.

Potential structures

Depending on the client’s objectives and subject to advice from appropriately qualified Luxembourg legal and tax advisers, potential solutions may include the structures listed here.

The choice of vehicle, and any regulatory authorisation it requires, is always determined with those advisers and the relevant authorised providers.

  • Luxembourg sociétés anonymesS.A.
  • S.à r.l. and other commercial companies
  • Holding and investment structures
  • SOPARFI structures
  • Special-purpose and acquisition vehicles
  • RAIFs and other alternative investment fund structures
  • Regulated investment vehicles, where appropriate

How the process works

  1. Discovery and objectives

  2. Preliminary structuring assessment

  3. Provider selection and coordination

  4. Establishment and onboarding

  5. Operational launch

  6. Ongoing strategic and governance support
